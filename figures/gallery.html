Embryonic tissues as active foams

Side-by-side reproduction of Kim, Pochitaloff, Stooke-Vaughan & Campàs, Nature Physics 17, 859–866 (2021). doi:10.1038/s41567-021-01215-1. Each card shows the published panel (left) beside the Python reproduction (right).

The reference MATLAB model is ported to NumPy across three complementary implementations: a faithful vertex-network foam model with curved contacts (intermediate vertices) and extracellular spaces; a confluent dynamic vertex model for the dynamics; and a deformable-particle and soft-particle jamming model for structure. A stochastic simulation can't be reproduced bit-for-bit — the goal is the paper's quantitative results: the curves, scaling exponents, and transition values.
